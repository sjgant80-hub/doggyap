What this is. Roughly a fifth of how a dog communicates is sound. The rest is body — tail, posture, ears, gaze, hackles, mouth. Every axis votes, the votes are fused, and when they disagree that disagreement is shown rather than smoothed away. A stiff high wag with a hard stare and forward weight is a warning, not joy, and only the other axes can tell you which.

What it is not. It produces no words. There is no sentence, no caption, nothing put in the dog's mouth — the output is a state from a fixed set, a confidence score, and the axes that drove it. It is not a diagnosis: readings consistent with pain raise a vet flag and nothing more. It is a reader, not a mind.

Measured versus observed. Voice and tail motion are measured from the microphone and camera and are marked measured. Tail height, ears, gaze, hackles, posture and mouth need a dog-pose keypoint model that this does not ship, so you tap those yourself. The fusion — the part that catches the misreads — works the same either way.

Everything stays on this device: the profile is in local storage, no video or audio leaves the browser, and it works offline. Kernel: doggyap.mjs, mutation-gated, 56 tests.
